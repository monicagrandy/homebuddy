Privacy Policy

Home Buddy · Effective October 2026

Home Buddy helps you keep your home's manuals, warranties and other documents in one place and answers questions about them. This policy explains what we collect, why, who processes it, how long we keep it, and how to delete it. It covers the Home Buddy website (homebuddy.chat) and the Home Buddy apps.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and name from sign-in, and the identifier of your sign-in account.
  • Household details: the household name, zip code and home type you enter.
  • Documents you add: PDF files you upload, links you add (including links scanned from QR codes), and the text we extract from them to answer questions. Before that text is indexed, we remove some personal identifiers it contains: Social Security numbers, card numbers, bank account numbers and email addresses.
  • Questions and answers: your chat history. The same identifiers are removed from questions before they are saved.
  • Voice input (apps): when you dictate a question, your phone's speech recognition turns it into text. On supported phones this happens on the device; otherwise your phone's speech service (for example Google) processes the audio. Home Buddy only receives the resulting text, which you review before sending.
  • Technical information: the app version and standard server logs (such as IP address and request time) used to keep the service running and secure.

We don't sell your information, show ads, or use your documents to train AI models.

How we use it

  • To answer your questions from your own documents and show which documents an answer came from.
  • To keep your documents and chats in sync across the devices you sign in on.
  • To detect safety hazards in questions (for example a gas smell) and show safety guidance.
  • To protect the service from abuse and fix problems.

Who processes it

  • Amazon Web Services hosts Home Buddy and runs sign-in (Amazon Cognito).
  • OpenAI generates answers, builds the search index for your documents, and checks content for safety. Passages from your documents and your questions are sent to OpenAI's API for this. OpenAI doesn't use API data to train its models, and keeps it for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring.
  • LangSmith (by LangChain) may be used to monitor the quality of AI requests. When it is, it receives the requests and answers involved.
  • Optional features that are off by default, such as web search and contractor search, send your question to their providers only when turned on.

How long we keep it

  • Chats are deleted automatically after 90 days without activity, or sooner when you delete them.
  • Documents are kept until you delete them or your account.
  • Records of document uploads are deleted 30 days after processing finishes.
  • Server backups are kept for a limited time and then deleted.

Security

Connections to Home Buddy use HTTPS. Uploaded files are encrypted at rest, and only you, from your signed-in devices, can see your household's documents and your chats.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account and all of its data at any time:

  • In the app: Account → Delete account.
  • On the web: homebuddy.chat/?page=delete-account, then My Profile → Delete account.

This deletes your household, its documents and your chat history on every device, then closes your sign-in account. You can also delete individual documents and chats at any time.

Children

Home Buddy isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We'll update this page when our practices change and change the effective date above. Significant changes will be announced in the app.
