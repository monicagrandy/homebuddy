Home Buddy · Account and data deletion

You can delete your Home Buddy account and all of its data at any time.

In the app

Open Account → Delete account and confirm.

Without the app

Email [add a contact email address before publishing] from the email address you sign in with, and ask us to delete your account. We'll confirm once it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your household, if you're its only member, with its documents, the text indexed from them and any uploaded files.
  • Your chat history, on every device.
  • Your sign-in account.

Server backups are kept for a limited time and then deleted, as described in the privacy policy. You can also delete individual documents and chats at any time without deleting your account.
